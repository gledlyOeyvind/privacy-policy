# Tempora Romae — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 10 May 2026

## Overview

Tempora Romae is a Roman calendar app that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

## Location Data

The app requests access to your device's location solely to calculate sunrise and sunset times for temporal hour display. Your location is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to any server or third party.

The app stores your most recent coordinates locally on your device (in shared app storage) so that widgets and complications can display temporal hours without requiring a new location request. This data never leaves your device.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

## Data Collection

Tempora Romae does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

## Widgets and Complications

Home screen widgets and Apple Watch complications access shared preferences stored locally on your device (such as display settings and cached location). No data is transmitted externally.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
